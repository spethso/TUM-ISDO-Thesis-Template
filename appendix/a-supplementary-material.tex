\chapter{Supplementary Material}\label{chap:appendix:supplementary}
% =================================================================================

Material that supports your thesis but would interrupt the reading flow belongs in the appendix, e.g., interview guides, questionnaires, additional tables and figures, or detailed results.
Every appendix must be referenced from the main text.

\section{Questionnaire}\label{sec:appendix:questionnaire}

Even sections are possible in the appendix.
