\section{Evaluation}\label{sec:expose:evaluation}

Describe how you plan to evaluate your own results.
For each research question, state what you will measure and how, e.g., with the \gls{gqm} approach~\cite{Basili1994}:
Which goals do you want to reach, which questions tell whether you reached them, and which metrics answer these questions?
Describe the planned evaluation method, e.g., a user study, a controlled experiment, case studies, or a performance benchmark, and the data or participants you need.
Also consider threats to validity~\cite{Runeson2009}.
