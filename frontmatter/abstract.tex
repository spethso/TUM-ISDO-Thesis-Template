\todo[inline]{\textbf{Structure, not format.}
  The labels \emph{Context}, \emph{Problem}, \emph{Objective}, \emph{Method}, \emph{Results}, and \emph{Conclusion} show the structure your abstract should follow.
  You do not have to use them as explicit labels: you can also write the abstract as continuous text, as long as it covers these parts in this order.}

\textit{Context.}
Which area of computer science is your thesis about, and why does it matter?

\textit{Problem.}
Which problem do you tackle, and why has nobody adequately solved it yet?

\textit{Objective.}
What is the objective of your thesis, i.e., your research question?

\textit{Method.}
How did you approach the problem?
What did you build, measure, or study?

\textit{Results.}
What are your main results?

\textit{Conclusion.}
What do the results mean, and who benefits from them?
